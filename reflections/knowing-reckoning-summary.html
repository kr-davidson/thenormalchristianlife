Knowing and
Reckoning

✦

A simple guide to one of the most important ideas in the Christian life: the difference between knowing a truth in your head and knowing it in your heart, and what to do when you cannot seem to cross from one to the other.

Romans 6:11

In the same way, count yourselves dead to sin but alive to God in Christ Jesus.

The Big Idea in Plain Language

When Jesus died on the cross, something remarkable happened. Not only did He take the punishment for your sins, He also took you with Him into that death. And when He rose from the dead, you rose with Him into a new life. This is what Romans 6 is all about.

The practical question is: how does that truth go from being something I believe to something I actually live? Watchman Nee spent seven years trying to figure this out. His answer involves two steps: knowing and reckoning.

Step One
Knowing

More than reading the Bible verse. It is when the truth becomes real and vivid to you personally, like a light coming on inside. The Spirit opens the eyes of your heart to see what has always been true.

Step Two
Reckoning

Choosing to count as true what God has declared. Not pretending. Not positive self-talk. Simply agreeing with what God says about you, even when your feelings have not yet caught up.

✦   ✦   ✦

Two Kinds of Knowing

There is a real difference between these two kinds of knowing. Both are genuine, but only one changes how you actually live.

Type What it looks like What it produces
Head You can explain Romans 6 clearly. You know what co-crucifixion with Christ means. You understand the doctrine. You know it, but it does not change how you respond when you are tempted or discouraged.
Heart The truth has become vivid and real to you personally. You do not just believe it for everyone else. You see that it is true for you. Reckoning becomes natural, almost effortless. You start to live from the truth rather than straining to maintain it.
Nee's Key Insight

You cannot manufacture heart knowledge by trying harder. It comes when the Holy Spirit makes the truth personally real to you. But you can position yourself to receive it by keeping your heart genuinely turned toward God.

✦   ✦   ✦

Why Reckoning Often Does Not Work

For seven years, Nee told himself "I am dead to sin" over and over. Nothing changed. He was reckoning without first truly knowing. Here is the difference between the two approaches.

Wrong Reckoning Right Reckoning
Repeating a truth hoping it will feel true Agreeing with what God has already declared
Trying to convince yourself by willpower Trusting the Word, not your feelings
Focused on your own interior state Focused on Christ, not yourself
Treating reckoning as the engine Reckoning flows from seeing
Monitoring how well it is working Looking to God, not inward
✦   ✦   ✦

Where Is Your Heart Pointed?

The biggest reason people get stuck between head knowledge and heart knowledge is not a lack of effort. It is the direction their heart is facing. A heart turned toward God is positioned to receive revelation. A heart focused on itself keeps the truth at arm's length.

Heart pointed toward What this looks like What tends to happen
Self Measuring truth by how you feel. Trusting your own self-assessment more than God's Word. Truth stays in the head. Reckoning feels empty. You feel like the exception the Bible did not mean.
God Letting God's declaration outweigh your own experience. Willing to be wrong about yourself. You become open to revelation. The Spirit has room to work. Truth starts to move from the head into the heart and life.
A Prayer to Try

"Lord, I am willing to let what You say about me be more true than what I feel about myself. Show me what You see."

✦   ✦   ✦

What Might Be Getting in the Way

If the truth of Romans 6 still feels distant, it often comes down to one or more of these honest conditions. Many of them can operate simultaneously.

Condition What it feels like A step forward
Still learning "I am not sure I fully understand what Romans 6 is even saying." Keep reading and asking. Understanding comes first. That is completely normal.
Contrary belief "I believe it for other people, but I cannot see how it applies to me specifically." Name this honestly before God. Ask Him to show you why you feel like an exception.
Known disobedience There is something in your life you know is wrong but are not ready to give up. Bring it to God. You do not have to fix it alone first. Just name it.
Too much inward looking "I am constantly checking whether the truth is working in me yet." Turn your attention outward to God. The monitoring itself can become the obstacle.
Waiting patiently "I understand it, I want it, I keep showing up, but it has not clicked yet." Stay faithful. The Spirit works on His own schedule. Your seeking is not wasted.
✦   ✦   ✦

What You Can Actually Do

You cannot force revelation, but you can stay positioned for it.

  • 1
    Read Romans 6 slowly and repeatedly. Not to memorise it for an exam. Read it asking God to make it real to you personally. Pray Ephesians 1:18 as you read: "Open the eyes of my heart."
  • 2
    Turn your heart toward God before you reckon. A few moments of honest prayer before engaging with the truth: "I want Your word to be more real to me than my own feelings right now."
  • 3
    Reckon anyway, even when it feels thin. Romans 6:11 is a command, not a reward for the spiritually advanced. You count what is already in the account. Act on it by faith, not by feeling.
  • 4
    Be honest about what might be in the way. Is there a known area of disobedience? A part of your life you are holding back from God? Bring it into the open. You do not need to fix it first, just name it.
  • 5
    Let others help you see yourself. Talk about this with someone in your church or small group. Other people can sometimes see what we cannot see in ourselves. You were not meant to do this alone.
  • 6
    Trust that your longing is already grace. The fact that you want this to be real is itself a sign that God is at work. Augustine wrote: "Our heart is restless until it rests in You." That restlessness is not a problem. It is God calling you forward.

What the Bible Actually Says

"Our old self was crucified with Him so that the body ruled by sin might be done away with, that we should no longer be slaves to sin."
Romans 6:11
"Count yourselves dead to sin but alive to God in Christ Jesus."
Ephesians 1:17–18
"I keep asking that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ may give you the Spirit of wisdom and revelation, so that the eyes of your heart may be enlightened."
Galatians 2:20
"I have been crucified with Christ and I no longer live, but Christ lives in me. The life I now live in the body, I live by faith in the Son of God."

The Whole Thing in Three Sentences

Remember This
Your death with Christ is a fact that does not depend on how you feel about it.
The Holy Spirit's job is to make that fact real and vivid to you personally.
Your job is to keep your heart turned toward God, stay engaged with His Word,
and count as true what He has already declared, trusting that the seeing will come.
A Word of Encouragement

If you are frustrated that this is not clicking yet, that frustration is a sign that you care. God is not frustrated with you. Keep showing up.
